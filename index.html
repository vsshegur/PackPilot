Complete seller toolkit

Every step after the order arrives.

Use only the tools you need today. Saved SKU costs and account defaults connect the workflow, so repeated work becomes faster.

01
Flipkart & Meesho Label Cutter

Upload several marketplace PDFs, detect individual orders, keep exchange-order SKUs intact, and export sharp thermal layouts with clean bottom borders.

  • 3×5, 4×4 and 4×6 sizes
  • Label-only or label + invoice
  • Automatic courier, seller, SKU and quantity summary
02
Brand logo & store QR

Save one account logo for every supported size. Meesho store links are matched to “Sold By” and printed as a QR without covering shipment details.

03
Private Seller Dashboard

Each Seller sees only their own Flipkart and Meesho order totals, pieces, processing history and recent label batches. Other sellers cannot access this data.

04
Meesho Profit & Loss

Combine one order month with multiple later payment files and the six-month return sheet. PackPilot matches Order and Sub Order IDs, detects period ad spend when available, and deducts ads before showing final profit.

05
Settlement Reconciliation

Compare the order report with the settlement report by order ID. See matched, missing, short-paid and excess-paid transactions immediately.

06
Master & child SKU mapping

Group several marketplace SKU names under one packing identity. Flipkart and Meesho lists stay separate, while saved product, packaging and labour costs flow into profit tools.

07
Price & Margin Calculator

Test a selling price against product cost, marketplace fees, shipping, ads, GST and expected returns before you list.

08
Six-hour Cloud PDF Desk

The Seller chooses only from label PDFs processed in PackPilot during the previous six hours. Random file uploads are not available. Only that Seller and their assigned Operations Manager can open the shared files.

  • Preview, download and print from another device
  • Visible local and cloud expiry countdowns
  • Seller can permanently delete any file earlier
